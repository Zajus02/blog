**Explorations** is an album I created under the _Directionless Vector_ name in 2021. It was a solo project created me to allow to test myself in all roles surrounding creation of music - from composing, writing lyrics, recording, programming, through mixing, mastering and publishing. After years of listening to music with critical ear, I opened myself to critical ear of others.

The music is built on eclectic tastes developed during several decades of explorations of all realms music has to offer. Inspired by the wonderful music of Thy Catafalque, Porcupine Tree, Der Weg Einer Freiheit, Nils Frahm, Anathema or Sigh, the music is an intentional blend and seeming meandering accorss my many music inspirations.
